## Interpretation and precision

Source dimensions, counts, ages and coordinates are authoritative in MODELING_GUIDE.md and ART_REFERENCE_SOURCE.md. Raster concept drawings illustrate construction and composition; incidental image labels, perspective and decorative background detail do not replace the source measurements. Proposed house-owner identities, cultural alternatives, the optional bridge, the spirit echo and UI layouts are design interpretations. The files are visual references, rather than game-ready meshes or executable effects.

## Delivery

Every board has a 3840×2160 PNG, a lightweight 960×540 gallery preview, a recorded generation prompt and native-resolution metadata. Native generation is 1672 pixels wide and 940–941 pixels high in this set; delivery PNGs are upscaled. This is explicitly recorded, rather than presented as native 4K rendering.
